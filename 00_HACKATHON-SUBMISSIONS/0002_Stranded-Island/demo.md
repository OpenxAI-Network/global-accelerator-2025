# 🎮 Stranded Island Adventure - Live Demo Guide

## 🚀 Quick Start Demo

### 1. Start the Game
```bash
# Navigate to the project directory
cd 00_HACKATHON-SUBMISSIONS/0002_Stranded-Island

# Use the start script (recommended)
./start.sh                    # macOS/Linux
# OR
start.bat                     # Windows

# The game will open at: http://localhost:3000
```

### 2. Experience the Complete Story

#### 🌅 **Opening Scene**
- You wake up on a sandy beach, confused and with no memory
- The AI presents you with 4 initial choices
- Choose your first action to begin the adventure

#### 🧭 **Exploration Phase**
- Search the island for clues and survival resources
- Make choices that affect your health and inventory
- Discover the island's secrets and mysteries

#### 🔍 **Discovery Phase**
- Find evidence of another person on the island
- Uncover clues about what happened to you
- Progress through the story milestones

#### 👥 **Encounter Phase**
- Meet another survivor or mysterious figure
- Your previous choices determine the interaction
- Navigate the relationship dynamics

#### 🏁 **Resolution Phase**
- Reach one of multiple possible endings
- Your choices, inventory, and relationships matter
- Experience the consequences of your decisions

## 🎯 Demo Scenarios

### Scenario 1: The Cautious Explorer
- Choose safe, methodical options
- Focus on survival and gathering resources
- Build trust slowly with any encounters
- **Expected Outcome**: Survival-focused ending with moderate success

### Scenario 2: The Bold Adventurer
- Take risks and explore dangerous areas
- Make quick decisions without full information
- Be aggressive or confrontational in encounters
- **Expected Outcome**: High-risk, high-reward ending

### Scenario 3: The Diplomatic Survivor
- Balance survival with relationship building
- Make morally conscious choices
- Seek peaceful resolutions to conflicts
- **Expected Outcome**: Cooperative ending with strong relationships
